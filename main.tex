\begin{abstract}
Streaming dance must prepare for music that has not yet arrived while continuing
from motion that can no longer be revised. We present \method{}, which uses a
frozen music foundation model to forecast future music states from arrived
audio and condition robot-native dance generation. A kinematics-guided
structure--detail representation and paired reconstruction objectives give
whole-body structure and local detail complementary roles. \cof{} trains
continuation from jointly committed structure and detail and the reference
state reconstructed from their decoded motion. The framework repeatedly plans and commits
motion, with a reference interface to a fixed whole-body controller.
On 198 generated 60-second FineDance trajectories, a frozen music--motion
evaluator favors the intended soundtrack over alternative gallery tracks in
74.20\% and 75.01\% of pairwise comparisons under two startup settings, with
positive margins across all generator training seeds. These results support
soundtrack correspondence in the generated references. ForeDance connects
musical foresight, structured motion generation, and learning to continue from
committed movement within a causal streaming framework.
\end{abstract}
