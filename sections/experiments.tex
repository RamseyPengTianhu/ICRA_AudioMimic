\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}
Our evaluation distinguishes musical correspondence, motion quality, and
execution. The completed studies characterize forecast-conditioned reference
motion and supporting tracking behavior. Section~\ref{sec:evidence_scope}
identifies the comparisons still needed to attribute the core method's gains.

\subsection{Setup and Evaluation}
\label{sec:experimental_setup}
\label{sec:evaluation_protocol}
The correspondence study contains 198 generated 60\,s FineDance trajectories
in native G1 space: eleven tracks, two startup settings, three independently
trained generators and three sampling seeds per generator. We call the
settings \emph{prefix-aware startup} and \emph{direct-prefix startup}; the
study characterizes both without selecting a winner. All associated tracks
form a fixed music gallery, with their original audio offsets retained.
A separate motion-quality study uses the full archived 18-track cohort.
Training settings and the distinct cohort protocols are specified in
Appendices~\ref{app:training} and~\ref{app:scope}.

\paragraph{Music--motion correspondence}
Motivated by music--motion retrieval~\cite{li2025soulnet}, we use a frozen
G1-native evaluator trained independently of the generator. Its score
$s_w(x,a)$ averages cosine similarities from three evaluator instances for
five-second music--motion windows. For intended music $a_i$ and another
gallery track $a_j$, we compute
\begin{equation}
\delta_{ijw}=s_w(x_i,a_i)-s_w(x_i,a_j).
\label{eq:mmr_margin}
\end{equation}
Pairwise correspondence accuracy counts positive margins, with half credit
for ties; random ordering gives 50\%. This measures preference for the intended
soundtrack while motion is fixed, rather than eleven-way retrieval accuracy
or overall dance quality. All ten alternative tracks, windows and sampling
repeats contribute. Descriptive 95\% intervals resample training seeds and
query tracks with the gallery fixed; overlapping windows are not treated as
independent samples. Appendix~\ref{app:metric_construction} gives the evaluator
and its calibration, and Appendix~\ref{app:scope} gives aggregation details.

\paragraph{Motion quality}
Kinetic/geometric Fr\'echet distances and diversity characterize generated
motion distributions. Root/contact proxies and jerk complement these measures
with grounding and smoothness information. The 18-track results are analyzed
within their own cohort rather than pooled with soundtrack correspondence.

\subsection{Soundtrack Correspondence and Generated Motion}
\label{sec:matching_quality}
Table~\ref{tab:mmr_correspondence} shows consistent preference for intended
music. Correct-pair cosine exceeds wrong-track cosine by 0.1786 and 0.1783
for prefix-aware and direct-prefix startup, respectively. Correct music wins
74.20\% and 75.01\% of pairwise comparisons. Every generator training seed has
a positive mean margin in both settings, supporting correspondence across
independently trained models in this gallery.

\paragraph{Complementary motion quality}
The 18-track study examines FHC as a supporting training choice. Distribution
distances improve most under mixed-start training, while contact and jerk
favor clean history. The full values in Appendix~\ref{app:fhc} document this
distribution--smoothness tradeoff alongside the correspondence results.

\subsection{Supporting Execution Diagnostics}
\label{sec:execution_results}
The interface in Section~\ref{sec:robot_execution} separates the generated,
resampled, controller-consumed and measured trajectories. The supporting study replays four saved trajectories from one FineDance track
through fixed SONIC in MuJoCo. These trajectories use an earlier 34-dimensional
representation, separate from the current 38-dimensional generation cohort;
audio inference is not run during replay.

After excluding the first two seconds and aligning joint trajectories, median
amplitude retention ranges from 0.858 to 0.901, and median squared-velocity
retention from 0.411 to 0.496. Position tracking therefore coexists with dynamic
attenuation. These diagnostics characterize the reference-to-execution path;
they do not establish current-model hardware performance or comparative
musical benefit. Appendix~\ref{app:execution} retains the complete measurements
and protocol.
